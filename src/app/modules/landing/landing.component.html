Our Story

Born from a Purpose,
Built with Heart

ALZCARE was born from witnessing firsthand the challenges families face when a loved one is diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease. The fragmented care, the missed medications, the sleepless nights wondering if everything is okay.

We set out to build a platform that bridges the gap between patients, their families, caregivers, and medical professionals — creating a single, unified space where everyone involved in the care journey can collaborate seamlessly.

Our mission is simple: no patient should feel alone, and no caregiver should feel overwhelmed. Every feature we build is guided by empathy and the belief that technology should serve human connection, not replace it.
